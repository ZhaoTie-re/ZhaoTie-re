## Zhao Tie

PhD student, Graduate School of Medicine, Kyoto University.

I work on statistical genetics with whole-genome sequencing data. My work runs from sequencing data to association results. It covers:
- sample and variant quality control;
- ancestry inference;
- common- and rare-variant association;
- HLA typing and association;
- the integration of published evidence.

I write the analyses as Nextflow pipelines for HPC clusters, in Python and R.

**Selected repositories**

- [cteph_agp3k.v6](https://github.com/ZhaoTie-re/cteph_agp3k.v6): a whole-genome sequencing case–control analysis. It covers quality control, principal components, a common-variant GWAS (PLINK 2, SAIGE), rare-variant gene tests and HLA association.
- [cteph_knowledge_graph](https://github.com/ZhaoTie-re/cteph_knowledge_graph): a dated knowledge graph of disease genetics and related conditions, built from Open Targets, published GWAS and PubMed.
- [PopGMM](https://github.com/ZhaoTie-re/PopGMM): ancestry inference and sample selection with PCA and Gaussian mixture models.
- [soma_qc](https://github.com/ZhaoTie-re/soma_qc): quality control of SomaScan proteomics data.

**Tools:** Nextflow, Python, R, PLINK 2, REGENIE, SAIGE, SLURM.
